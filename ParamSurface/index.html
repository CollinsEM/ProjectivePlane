Parametric Surface Explorer

A surface patch is a map from a 2-D parameter domain into space: (u, v) ∈ [0,1]² ↦ S(u, v) = (x, y, z). Drag the point in the little param-domain box (bottom-right of the 3-D view) and watch the matching point glide over the surface. A curve had one parameter (a slider); a surface has two — its domain is a square.

A parametric patch is a function S(u, v) from the unit square into ℝ³. The u- and v-iso-lines through the current point are the surface's coordinate curves; the tangent plane is spanned by the partials Su, Sv and has normal Su × Sv. For a tensor-product Bézier patch the surface is the projection of a polynomial net of control points Pij; giving the nodes weights makes it a rational patch — a polynomial patch in homogeneous ℝ⁴ projected down, exactly the idea of the Projective Plane demo (a rational Bézier curve there becomes a rational Bézier surface here).
