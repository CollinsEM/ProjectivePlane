The projective plane is the set of lines through the origin of ℝ³:  [x : y : w] = [λx : λy : λw], λ ≠ 0. The right pane draws the affine chart w = 1; points of the curve with w < 0 are drawn through their antipodal representative −p (dashed), so every projective point except ℓ∞ = {[x : y : 0]} gets a finite spot. Watch a hyperbola: it leaves the chart through one point at infinity and comes back through the antipodal one — in ℝP² it is a closed loop. Equivalently, ℝP² is the sphere S² with antipodal points identified (S²/±1).
