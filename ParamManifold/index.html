Projective Manifold Explorer

A surface (u, v) ↦ (x, y, w) in 3D homogeneous space ℝ³ projects through the origin onto the 2D projective plane ℝP². Every point p and its antipode −p (same ray [λx : λy : λw]) become one projective point; points with w = 0 fall on the line at infinity.

A 2-parameter surface S(u, v) = (x, y, w) lives in homogeneous ℝ³. Projecting each point through the origin onto the chart plane w = 1 gives the affine point (a, b) = (x/w, y/w) of ℝP². The left pane draws the surface in (x, y, w); the right pane draws its shadow in the projective chart. The surface is coloured by the sign of w: teal = w > 0 (upper representative), magenta = w < 0 (charted through the antipode — the same projective point). Where w → 0 the chart runs off to the line at infinity ℓ∞ (the dashed window border). The sphere preset is the classic picture: ℝP² = S² with antipodal points identified (S²/±1).
